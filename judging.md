# Judging

## When

- Submissions are due Sunday, August 23 at 12 PM (Argentina time).
- Judging runs Sunday, August 23, 1 PM–5 PM (Argentina time).
- Closing Ceremony streamed Sunday at 5 PM (Argentina time).

## How

- Judging is conducted async, entirely online, in English or Spanish (with English captions).
- Submit a 3-minute demo video showcasing your project.
- Judges and bounty partners review all submissions and select the winning projects.
- Prize payouts are processed within 30–60 days after winners are announced, operated directly by the sponsors themselves (not Crecimiento).

## Criteria

Check the criteria of the sponsors' bounties you're applying to (see the sponsor's dedicated section on Hacki). Projects are judged on:

### Technicality

- What technical challenges does the project address?
- Does it demonstrate advanced problem-solving and engineering skills?
- Is the solution complete and functional, or does the team have a clear plan to finish it?

### Originality

- Is the project innovative and unique?
- Does it address specific pain points?
- Does it propose a new solution or a creative approach to an existing problem?

### UI/UX/DX

- How user-friendly and accessible is the project?
- Is the user interface, user experience, and developer experience well-designed and intuitive?

### Practicality

- How practical and feasible is the project?
- Does it have real-world applications, and can it be implemented effectively?

### Presentation

- Is the project clearly explained and well-presented?
- Does the team communicate their idea effectively through their pitch, demo, and supporting materials?
